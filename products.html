Unified Care

Washing Synergy: One Simple Routine for All Reusables

Parents often ask if each product needs special handling. The answer is no! All PureNest reusable essentials—diapers, wipes, bibs, burp cloths, and nursing pads—wash together in standard family laundry.

Step 01 • Storage

Airy Dry Pail Storage

Toss used cloth diapers, wet wipes, soiled bibs, and nursing breast pads into an open-air basket or hanging wet bag. Good airflow prevents ammonia odors naturally without soaking buckets.

  • Zero soaking buckets needed
  • Wash every 2 to 3 days
  • Natural airflow odor control
Step 02 • Pre-Wash

Warm Pre-Wash Cycle

Run a quick 30–45 minute warm cycle (40°C–60°C) with standard laundry detergent. This flushes away 95% of urine, food purees, and milk soiling before the main deep wash.

  • 30-45 min quick cycle
  • Regular family detergent
  • Flushes 95% surface soiling
Step 03 • Deep Clean

Main Clean & Sun Drying

Run a thorough heavy cottons wash. Line-dry bamboo wipes, cotton inserts, and burp cloths in natural sunlight to bleach stains, or tumble dry inserts on low heat.

  • Heavy cotton cycle deep wash
  • Sunlight naturally lifts stains
  • Safe for tumble drying low
