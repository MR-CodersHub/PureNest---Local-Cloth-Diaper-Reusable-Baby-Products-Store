Why We Started

Sustainable Baby Care Beyond Diapers

When our first baby was born in 2021, we were shocked not only by how many disposable diapers filled our outdoor bins, but by the sheer volume of single-use wet wipe packets, plastic nursing pads, and paper towels we discarded every single week.

Globally, over 20 billion disposable diapers and an estimated 100 billion single-use wipes containing polyester microplastics end up in landfills each year, persisting for over 500 years.

When we replaced disposables with washable bamboo cloth wipes, one-size cloth diapers, organic bandana bibs, and washable nursing pads, our nursery was transformed. Our baby's painful rashes healed, our garbage bins emptied, and we saved thousands of dollars. PureNest was founded to bring that same joyful simplicity to every Australian parent.

Realistic Parenting

Zero Guilt, 100% Real Support

We don't believe in rigid rules or perfectionism. Every single reusable item you choose makes a genuine difference.

Our Core Belief

Progress Over Perfection

Many brands make parents feel like they have to use cloth 100% of the time, 24/7, or they’re failing. That is nonsense! If you use cloth wipes at home and disposables when traveling, that is amazing. If you use cloth diapers during the daytime and a disposable at night, you still divert thousands of single-use items.

We celebrate whatever level of reusable baby care fits your lifestyle, mental health, and family schedule.
