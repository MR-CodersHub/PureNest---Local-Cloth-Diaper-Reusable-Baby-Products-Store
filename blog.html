Nursery Wash & Care Cheat Sheet

High-Resolution Printable Vector Guide • 100% Free
PRINTABLE NURSERY GUIDE

PureNest Daily Wash & Care Protocol

Keep this visual guide pinned near your machine for simple, odor-free, non-toxic washing.

STEP 1: PRE-WASH

Short Warm Cycle

Temp: 40°C • Time: 30–45 mins
Detergent: 1/2 regular scoop

Runs every 1–2 days. Removes 95% of urine and soiling so the main wash is clean water sanitization.

STEP 2: MAIN WASH

Long Heavy Cottons

Temp: 40°C–60°C • Time: 2–2.5 hours
Detergent: Full standard dose

Runs every 2–3 days. Thorough mechanical friction and water flow sanitizes diapers, wipes, and bibs.

STEP 3: DRYING

Air Line Dry / Low Dryer

Covers: Line dry in shade
Inserts & Wipes: Sunshine or low tumble

Natural ultraviolet sunshine safely bleaches stains without chemical optical brighteners.

Essential Laundry Golden Rules:
  • Never use fabric softeners: Coats fibers and ruins absorbency.
  • Never soak in water buckets: Encourages bacteria and degrades elastic.
  • Store in airy dry pails: Oxygen prevents ammonia odor buildup.
  • Wash all essentials together: Diapers, wipes, bibs wash in 1 drum.
